**📊 Executive Sales & Profit Dashboard**

One file. Ten charts. Every answer a CEO or CFO needs, filtered in a click.

An interactive, single-file HTML dashboard that turns 9,994 US retail order lines (2018–2021) into an executive-level view of revenue, profit, discounting, regions and logistics.

**✨ Features**

An interactive dashboard that turns 9,994 US retail order lines (2018–2021) into an executive view of revenue, profit, discounting, regions and logistics. One filter bar drives every chart, KPI and insight.


**🎛️ Global filter bar**

A sticky bar of dropdowns stays on screen while you scroll.

**🧮 KPI cards**

Revenue, profit, profit margin, order lines and unique customers, each with a ▲/▼ indicator against the prior year.

**💡 Automated insights**

Five callout cards (✓ success, ℹ info, ⚠ warning) that rewrite themselves from the current selection, such as best and worst category margin, discount exposure, regional leaders and laggards, loss-making sub-categories, and data-quality alerts.
