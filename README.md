Executive Sales & Profit Dashboard

An interactive, single-file HTML dashboard that turns 9,994 US retail order lines (2018–2021) into an executive-level view of revenue, profit, discounting, regions and logistics. Built for a CEO/CFO audience: every chart responds to one global filter bar, and a row of auto-generated insights updates as the filters change.

Features

- KPI bar: revenue, profit, profit margin, order lines and unique customers, each with a year-over-year indicator.

- Global filters: year, region, segment, category and ship mode. Every chart, KPI and insight updates together. A reset button clears all filters.

- Ten charts plus a heatmap:
1) Monthly sales and profit trend (dual axis)
2) Sales by category (donut, with % share)
3) Profit margin by sub-category (ranked; green above 15%, amber 0–15%, red when negative)
4) Regional performance (sales bars with margin % line)
5) Segment revenue by year (stacked bar)
6) Sales vs profit by sub-category (bubble size = quantity)
7) Discount impact on average profit
8) Top 10 states by revenue (coloured by region, margin label on each bar)
9) Ship mode mix with average days to ship and average profit per line
10) Annual sales and profit with YoY growth labels
11) Order calendar heatmap (month × day of month)

- Automated insights: five callout cards (success, info, warning) that recompute from the current selection.
- Data-quality toggle: excludes 11 flagged rows (see below).
- Light and dark mode, responsive layout, 200 ms update animations, hover tooltips with exact values.
